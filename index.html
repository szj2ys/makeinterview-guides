MakeInterview

A Chinese-first AI voice mock-interview tool. You are asked questions out loud and answer in the moment — the way a real interview works.

Disclosure: This guide collection is published by the makers of MakeInterview, the product it links to. It is a product companion, not an independent review.

Why it exists

Job seekers practice by reading question lists or rehearsing silently. Neither reproduces the one thing that transfers to the real room: being asked out loud and having to answer on the spot. MakeInterview runs a spoken interview and then shows you exactly what you said.

How the interviewer behaves

  • It follows up. The interviewer is modelled as a turn loop and may dig in once or twice before moving on, instead of waiting for a perfectly composed answer.
  • Questions are grounded. Every generated question anchors to a specific entity parsed from your resume and the target job description. If nothing can be anchored, it asks a clarification rather than inventing a plausible-sounding project.
  • Feedback is the transcript. Instead of a single score, you see your verbatim words next to a model answer — filler words and hedging included.
  • Five interviewer personas. An HR screener, a direct manager, a technical expert, a department head, and an executive. Same candidate, different pressure.

Design notes

The structure follows the evidence on selection methods: structured interviews rank among the highest-validity predictors of job performance (Schmidt & Hunter, 1998; the 2022 update by Sackett et al.), well ahead of unstructured conversation. So the interviewer stays structured rather than chatty.
